Usage

Please do

  • Use the supplied logo files as-is.
  • Keep clear space around the mark equal to the “Z” height.
  • Place the mark on a dark or plain background for best contrast.
  • Refer to the coin as ZKas and the ticker as ZKAS.

Please don't

  • Recolour, rotate, stretch or add effects to the logo.
  • Recreate the mark or pair it with another logo as one lockup.
  • Imply partnership or endorsement without written agreement.
  • Use the old “Firecash” name or marks.
