How to buy or sell ZKAS on the OTC desk

The desk is a simple marketplace: people post offers to swap ZKAS ↔ KAS, and other people take them. Prices are set by users, not by the project. It runs in the ZKas Discord — everything below happens in the /otc menu there.

Deposit: free Trade: free Withdraw: 3% fee

⚠First, the one rule that keeps you safe

We never DM you. Any deposit address sent to you in a direct message is a scam. Only use the address the /otc menu shows you, inside the server.

1Put coins on your desk

/otc → My Desk → Deposit → ZKAS or KAS

Send your coins to the address shown. Your balance appears by itself after a few confirmations — usually within a couple of minutes.

Buying ZKAS? Deposit KAS. Selling ZKAS? Deposit ZKAS.

2Trade

/otc → Trade → 🟢 Buy ZKAS or 🔴 Sell ZKAS

Buying spends the KAS on your desk; selling pays you in KAS. Two ways to do it:

  • Take an offer — pick one from the list. You get exactly the price shown, instantly. Easiest — start here.
  • Post my own offer — name your own price and wait for someone to take it. The coins for your offer are set aside from your balance until it fills or you cancel (My Orders → cancel anytime; the coins come straight back).
What’s a fair price? Open Trade Log and look at the last few fills before you accept or post anything.

3Take your coins home

/otc → My Desk → Withdraw

Enter your own wallet address. A 3% fee is taken from the amount — the only fee on the desk.

Coins on the desk are held by the desk. When you’re done trading, withdraw to your own wallet — that’s where they’re truly yours. Need a wallet? wallet.zkas.info.
